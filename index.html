Research

Kalinova's work is grounded in peer-reviewed research in X-ray astrophysics — especially the reliability and reproducibility of automated scientific analysis.

Expertise

  • Statistical inference on large, heterogeneous scientific datasets; HPC/SLURM computing.

Founder

Dr Karthik Balasubramaniam — Founder & Director

Karthik is an X-ray astrophysicist (Ph.D., Jagiellonian University, 2021) and former postdoctoral researcher at National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan (2022–2023). For a decade he has built and validated data-analysis pipelines for large scientific datasets, with recent work focused on how far automated scientific analysis can be trusted.

He founded Kalinova in 2026 to bring that research-grade rigour — reproducibility, validation, careful statistics — to software and data work for researchers and technical teams.

Python · scientific computing · reproducible research · software & web development. Personal site · ORCID · GitHub · LinkedIn
